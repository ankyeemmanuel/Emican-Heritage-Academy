About Emican Heritage Academy

Welcome to Emican Heritage Academy, a premier educational institution situated in Techiman, the vibrant regional capital of the Bono East Region, Ghana. Established with a commitment to academic excellence, discipline, and moral integrity, our school provides a supportive learning environment designed to empower children from early childhood through early adolescence. Our comprehensive educational programs span from Crèche and Kindergarten (KG 1 & 2) to Basic 1 through 6, culminating in Junior High School (J.H.S 1, 2, and 3).


At Emican Heritage Academy, we operate under the national curriculum standards set by the Ghana Education Service (GES), incorporating modern STEM concepts, arts, and character development. Guided by our foundational motto — ASCEND, EXPLORE, GROW — our dedicated teaching staff strives to inspire critical thinking, creative exploration, and high ethical values in every pupil. We believe that true education nurtures both intellect and character, equipping students with the confidence and skills necessary to excel academically and contribute meaningfully to Ghanaian society and the global community.


Our campus offers structured classrooms, dedicated learning spaces, and collaborative programs that engage students and parents alike. Through our integrated digital portal system, we foster seamless communication between administrators, teachers, and parents to monitor student progress, report card assessments, and school activities in real time.

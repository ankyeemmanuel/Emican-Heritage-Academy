Educational Heritage

Welcome to Emican Heritage Academy

Situated in the heart of Techiman, the vibrant commercial capital of the Bono East Region of Ghana, Emican Heritage Academy stands as a beacon of academic excellence and comprehensive youth development. Established with a firm commitment to nurturing young minds from early childhood to junior high school, our institution combines modern educational technologies with time-tested Ghanaian cultural values.

Our dedicated staff of qualified educators ensures that every child receives personalized attention in a safe, conducive, and stimulating learning environment. From our vibrant Crèche and Nursery programs through Kindergarten, Primary, and Junior High School (J.H.S.), we emphasize STEM learning, literacy, creative arts, and moral character.

At Emican Heritage Academy, we believe education is a collaborative journey involving teachers, students, and parents. We provide world-class facilities, practical learning experiences, and holistic co-curricular activities to prepare pupils for national BECE distinction and lifelong leadership.

About Emican Heritage Academy

Educational Excellence in Techiman

Emican Heritage Academy is a premier educational institution located in Techiman, the capital of the Bono East Region of Ghana. We offer comprehensive basic education from Creche 1 through JHS 3, focusing on academic rigor, character building, and cultural heritage.

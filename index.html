About Emican Heritage Academy

Techiman, Ghana

Welcome to Emican Heritage Academy, a premier educational institution located in Techiman, Bono East Region, Ghana. We are dedicated to nurturing young minds from Crèche through Junior High School (JHS 3).

Our holistic curriculum balances academic excellence with moral integrity, creative exploration, and physical development. Guided by our foundational motto — ASCEND, EXPLORE, GROW — we empower students to discover their potential and excel in a rapidly changing world.
